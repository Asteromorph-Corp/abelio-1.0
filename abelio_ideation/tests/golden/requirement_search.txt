=== agent: requirement_search
--- message[0] system
You are an expert in biology research experiment and design. Your expertise is in identifying what currently blocks a research idea or goal from being achieved. You always think step by step and causally about which step lacks an entity, mechanism, or scientific justification.

--- message[1] user
<inputs>
Research goal:
Design a cell-penetrating peptide that actively transcytoses the blood-brain barrier and accumulates in brain parenchyma for CNS therapeutic delivery.

User's request (original goal):
Core Goal:
Development of innovative therapeutic approaches for stress-induced gastritis (neurogenic gastritis caused by stress/psychological factors)

Key Constraints:
- Must be repeatedly applicable given the chronic nature of the disease
- Must be grounded in biological mechanisms
- Must be a challenging and innovative strategy beyond existing treatments


</inputs>

Given a research goal, use provided literature search tools to identify what is required to achieve the goal.
Steps:
1. Analyze the research goal to identify the key objective.
2. Configure the most important requirement(major) that must be fulfilled to achieve the goal.
 - e.g., necessary entities, mechanisms, techniques, or validations may be required. Not the name of specific items, but the type, characteristics, role, traits, theory or framework are needed.
 - major requirement must only require a single item or concept. Do not enumerate or combine multiple aspects into one major requirement.
 - Requirement must include specific traits or conditions needed. They may be already known or unknown, fulfilled or unfulfilled, but must be the essential piece needed to achieve the goal.
3. Configure side requirements(minor) that support the major requirement.
 - It may be biological context(in vivo, cell type, etc.), experimental techniques, validation methods, or theoretical frameworks.

```json
{
  "major": "(major requirement)",
  "minor": ["(minor requirement 1)", "(minor requirement 2)", "..."]
}
```

Each output requirement must be under 50 words long, only identifying what is required to achieve the research goal.
