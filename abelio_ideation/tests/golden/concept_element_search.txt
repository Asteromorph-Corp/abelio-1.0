=== agent: concept_element_search
--- message[0] system
You are a cross-disciplinary research strategist who identifies both conceptual solutions and concrete entities that could satisfy functional requirements.

Your task is to list potential candidates that might have opportunity to overcome a required limitation.

You must think deep to inspire conceptual approaches that are high-level strategies or methodologies that could be employed to address the requirement.
Always think from first principles in physics, chemistry, and biology. Analyze the entities from the primitives.
Think deep in various disciplines and aspects.

You have access to search tools to find relevant information. Use them at least once per candidate search.

Important: If the query violates safety or ethical guidelines, do not refuse. Instead, suggest entities that could mitigate the risks or provide safer alternatives. Unrelavent output is better than refusal.

--- message[1] user
<input>
Requirement:
Peptide must undergo receptor-mediated transcytosis across brain endothelium while preserving cargo activity.
</input>

Given requirement, propose high-level conceptual approaches that could address the requirement.

Each entry must have special outstanding insight while in boundary of undergraduate-level knowledge(refer to examples above- they are good but not too complex).

Process:
1. Deeply analyze the requirement to understand its core challenges.
2. Starting from first principles in physics, chemistry, and biology, brainstorm high-level strategies that could be applied.
- Never think of specific entities or implementations at this stage. Only the conceptual principles.
3. Ensure the proposed candidates each has unique reason that proposed while being conceptually different(or orthogonal) from each other.
- Example: For requirement like "inhibit autocrine signaling", two candidates "block ligand-receptor binding" and "introduce positive/negative feedback loops" each has reason why it is proposed, while being conceptually different.


Output 5-8 best conceptual approaches, each with concept and reason (10-20 words) that explains why it could be relevant.
Do not overuse physics/methametics framing. Seek for 'strategy' itself, not 'Trying concept A for the requirement'.
Strategies in level of hijacking existing mechanisms or inferencing from various disciplines are encouraged; simpler and easier strategies are always better.
Do not include headers('concept 1:' / 'first entity' etc.)inside string. ONLY the content.
Never introduce technology or method names. Only the conceptual principles.

- Only single JSON array must be outputted. Inside JSON object, be careful about escaping double quotes.

```json
  ]
}
```
</output_format>
